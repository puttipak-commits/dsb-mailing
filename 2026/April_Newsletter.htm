Thai TMO’s Expanding Fast-Track Routes: Strategic Speed for National Trademark Filings (APAA e-Newsletter: Issue No. 52, April 2026)


While the Madrid System offers centralized convenience, a growing backlog at the Thai Trademark Office has resulted in severe and unpredictable examination delays for Thailand (TH) designations. To meet the strict timelines of a dynamic commercial landscape, foreign applicants are increasingly pivoting back to direct national filings.


In this article published with the Asian Patent Attorneys Association (APAA), DS&B practitioners Padcha Ritkasem and Ariya Porchit outline the strategic advantages of the national route. The article details the latest fast-track options available at no additional official cost—including the 6-month, 4-month, and the newly introduced "4-month Plus+" scheme for e-commerce businesses. Trademarks in Thailand: 2025 Market Insights and 2026 Strategic Outlook


2025 marked a year of robust growth for trademark filings in Thailand, driven heavily by the booming retail, cosmetics, and wellness sectors. While the Thai Department of Intellectual Property (DIP) has made strides in procedural modernization, such as the introduction of e-certificates, international applications via the Madrid System continue to suffer from substantial and unpredictable prosecution delays. This annual review unpacks the latest market statistics and explains why leveraging direct national filings remains the most efficient and reliable strategy for global brands entering the Thai market.

Thailand and Japan Extend Patent Prosecution Highway (PPH) Program Through 2027


The Thai Department of Intellectual Property (DIP) and the Japan Patent Office (JPO) have officially announced an extension of the Patent Prosecution Highway (PPH) pilot program.


Both patent offices have committed to a further two-year term to facilitate the expedited examination of patent applications in Thailand and Japan. This renewed phase commenced on January 1, 2026, and remains in effect through December 31, 2027.
