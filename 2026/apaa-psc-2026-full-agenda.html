    How to properly construe a patent

    The fundamental rules of construction, including purposive construction, followed by practical examples of how these principles are applied. The role of expert evidence in construction.

    tutorBen Fitzpatrick

  6. 12:30-13:30
    Lunch
  7. 13:30-16:00

    topic 3

    Patent claim drafting exercise

    Participants draft claims to a patent specification in group exercises, assessed by the tutors. Each claim is checked to ensure it captures competing products, avoids unnecessary limitations, relates its elements to each other, overcomes the prior art and is industrially applicable. Particular focus on support and sufficiency, and the balance between broad claim scope and these internal requirements.

    tutorsSteven Fung, Ben Fitzpatrick, Greg Munt, Shigeru Inaba

    Recap of day one and outline of day two
